## About me

I'm a **Senior Mobile Developer** specialized in **Flutter and Dart**, with **10+ years in software** (since 2015) and **Flutter since 2019**. I deliver Android and iOS apps across the whole product lifecycle: architecture, UI, integrations, automated testing, CI/CD and store releases.

At **Cooper Tec** I work on the Flutter apps of a white-label financial platform; before that, I was at **Agrosmart** and **Toro Investimentos**. Outside client work I design, build and maintain my own products under **[Lucksrei](https://lucksrei.com)**, from the first prototype to the App Store.

I value clean architecture, pragmatic engineering decisions, resilient integrations and apps that feel right on real devices.
